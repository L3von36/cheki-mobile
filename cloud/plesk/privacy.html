Privacy Policy

Mahtem ማህተም — free Ethiopian bank receipt verification

The short version

Mahtem checks payment receipts on your device. The app has no advertising and no analytics. If you never create a cloud account, nothing about you ever leaves your phone except the receipt-check requests themselves, which go directly from your phone to your bank's official endpoint.

Zero-knowledge cloud: even a full compromise of Mahtem's server would leak no identifiers, no passwords and no readable history — by design, the server only ever stores cryptographic digests and encrypted ciphertext.

What stays on your device

  • Your account (identifier such as phone or email, and your password) is created and stored locally. Passwords are PBKDF2-hashed, and the account is kept in Android-Keystore-encrypted secure storage.
  • Your verification history lives in a local database on the phone.
  • The vault encryption key is derived from your password on the device and never leaves it.

What the receipt check sends — and to whom

When you verify a receipt, the app contacts the official public endpoint of the bank or wallet that issued it (for example CBE, EthioTelecom/Telebirr, Bank of Abyssinia or Safaricom M-Pesa) to read the record for that reference number. Mahtem's servers are not in that path. Only for Telebirr and M-Pesa, whose endpoints refuse some client IPs, may the app ask Mahtem's relay server to fetch the same official page on its behalf — the relay accepts only those two banks' receipt hosts, and nothing else.

The optional cloud account & backup

Creating a cloud account (for sign-in across devices and encrypted history backup) is entirely optional. If you do, this is all the server can ever see:

Data storedWhat it is
identifierHashSHA-256 digest of your normalized identifier. Your phone number or email is never sent or stored.
authKeyA PBKDF2-SHA256 key derived from your password on the device (120,000 iterations). Your raw password never reaches the server, and the stored key is not usable as a password anywhere else.
blobYour history backup, AES-256-GCM encrypted on the device. The server stores ciphertext it cannot decrypt.
receiptHashA SHA-256 fingerprint of a verified Telebirr invoice, used only to record that this month's Pro payment belongs to your account. Raw receipt data is never stored.

Mahtem Pro

Pro is an optional monthly subscription paid with a Telebirr transfer. To activate it, the app first verifies the payment receipt against EthioTelecom's official endpoint and then submits only the receipt's fingerprint. The server binds that fingerprint to your account, so one payment cannot be reused by another account. The verified receipts themselves are not stored by Mahtem.

Crash reports

Mahtem can collect crash reports so bugs can be fixed quickly. When enabled, crash reports contain technical diagnostics (such as device model, OS version and the error's stack trace). Crash reports never include your receipts, account data or history content.

What Mahtem never does

  • No advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of user data — there is no user data to sell.
  • No reading of your contacts, messages, photos or files beyond what you explicitly pick (a receipt screenshot you choose to scan).
  • No requirement to create an account in order to verify receipts.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — to scan receipt QR codes.
  • Photos/media (optional) — when you pick a saved receipt screenshot or PDF to verify.
  • Network — to reach the banks' official endpoints and, if you use it, the optional cloud service.
