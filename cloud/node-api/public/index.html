Why Mahtem

Trust, but verify — instantly

Fake payment screenshots are a real problem in Ethiopian marketplaces. Mahtem closes that gap: it fetches the receipt straight from the bank's public endpoint on your device and compares what you were shown with what the bank actually recorded.

📷

Scan or paste

Point your camera at a receipt QR code, or paste a reference number or link you were sent. Batch checking is built in.

🏦

Direct from the bank

No middleman re-typing your data — the app reads the official record on your device and tells you if the payment is genuine.

🔒

Private by design

Accounts and history stay on your phone, encrypted. The optional cloud backup stores only math — digests and ciphertext, never your raw data.

🌐

In your language

Fully localized in Amharic, Afaan Oromoo and Tigrinya, alongside English.

🧾

History & export

Every check is saved locally with its verdict, so you can look back, batch-audit and share results.

Small, fast and offline-graceful — built for real Ethiopian network conditions, not a demo.

How it works

Three steps to a stamped receipt

Scan

Open Mahtem and scan the QR code on the payment receipt — or paste the reference number / link you received.

Verify

Mahtem reads the official record from the bank and checks every detail: amount, parties, date and status.

Trust the stamp

Genuine payments get the ማህተም stamp with the full receipt details. Anything odd gets a clear warning — before you hand over the goods.

Your receipts belong to you

Verification happens on your device. If you choose to create a cloud account for backup, the server only ever stores cryptographic digests and encrypted ciphertext — it mathematically cannot read your identifiers, passwords or history.
